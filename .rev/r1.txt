But both engines implement the bound by counting *total events processed* rather than *self-raised chain depth*, which conflates a runaway loop with a busy queue.

- `task_done()` bookkeeping stays balanced on the async drop path — `_unfinished_tasks` returns to 0, `queue.join()` returns cleanly, no "task_done() called too many times", and the loop survives the drop.

- The underlying starvation bug is genuinely fixed.
